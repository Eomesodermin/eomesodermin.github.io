Wet lab and cell therapy

I engineer and assay primary human NK and CD8 T cells, design high-parameter flow cytometry panels, and run the preclinical models behind my analyses. Much of my work asks why cytotoxic lymphocytes fail in tumours and how to make cell therapies work better.

Techniques

Flow cytometry
Spectral and conventional flow cytometry with 40+ colour panels; FACS sorting and panel design.
Engineering
CRISPR/Cas9 in primary immune cells; CAR-NK and CAR-T engineering; lentiviral and adenoviral overexpression.
Human tissue
Primary human tissue and ex vivo models: organoids, air–liquid interface cultures, tissue fragments, CAM assays.
Functional assays
Cytotoxicity, intracellular cytokine staining, degranulation.
Preclinical models
Syngeneic tumour models and LCMV infection.

Immunology focus

Main questions
NK cell plasticity and dysfunction in tumours; CD8 T cell states and type I IFN-driven dysfunction; unconventional and tissue-resident T cells.
Settings
Anti-tumour and anti-viral immunity; translational and reverse-translational discovery; clinical immune phenotyping of patient tissue; biomarker development.
