What I run end to end

Single-cell
scRNA-seq, CITE-seq, scATAC-seq and scTCR-seq. CellRanger, STARsolo.
Spatial
Spatial transcriptomics and imaging.
Bulk and repertoire
Bulk RNA-seq (Salmon), TCR repertoire, WES. scRepertoire, immunarch.
Analysis
Harmony integration, pseudobulk differential expression, trajectories and RNA velocity, SCENIC, cell–cell communication (CellChat, NicheNet).
Lineage and structure
Single-cell VDJ and somatic mtDNA lineage tracing; protein structure prediction and de novo binder design (RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN, Boltz-2, Chai-1).
Pipelines and compute
Nextflow, Snakemake, SLURM, Apptainer on HPC; reproducible reports in Quarto.
Languages
R with the tidyverse and ggplot2; Python and applied machine learning.
