Single-cell and multi-omics analysis

Data types
scRNA-seq, CITE-seq, scATAC-seq and scTCR-seq; spatial transcriptomics (Visium) and high-multiplex imaging (CODEX/PhenoCycler); bulk RNA-seq and TCR-seq; exome and genome sequencing.
Processing and QC
CellRanger, STARsolo, Salmon/alevin-fry, kallisto-bustools; ambient RNA, doublet and empty-droplet removal (SoupX, CellBender, scDblFinder); hashtag and genotype demultiplexing.
Integration and annotation
Harmony, Seurat CCA/RPCA and weighted nearest neighbour; reference mapping and label transfer (Azimuth, CellTypist, SingleR, ProjecTILs).
Differential testing
Pseudobulk differential expression (DESeq2, edgeR, limma-voom); differential abundance (Milo); pathway and enrichment analysis (GSEA, decoupleR, PROGENy).
Trajectories and lineage
RNA velocity (scVelo, CellRank); pseudotime (Monocle, Slingshot); lineage tracing with TCR clones and mitochondrial DNA variants (mgatk, ReDeeM).
Gene regulation
Regulatory networks (SCENIC, DoRothEA); co-expression modules (WGCNA); chromatin accessibility with ArchR and Signac.
Immune repertoires
Clonotype calling (CellRanger VDJ, TRUST4, MiXCR), diversity and clonal tracking, CDR3 properties, specificity grouping (GLIPH, TCRdist), TCR embeddings (TCR-BERT).
Communication and space
Cell–cell communication (CellChat, NicheNet, LIANA); spatial neighbourhood and proximity analysis.
Other
Copy-number inference (inferCNV, CopyKAT); scVI and single-cell foundation models (scGPT, Geneformer).
